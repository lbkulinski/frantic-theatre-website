Drop past-production photos in this folder.

Naming: use a short slug per show, e.g. hamlet-2024-01.jpg, hamlet-2024-02.jpg
Size: photos display at roughly 4:3 — landscape shots work best. No need to
pre-crop; just avoid huge multi-megabyte files if possible (resize to ~1600px
wide max for faster page loads).

  - replace the <img src="..."> with the real filename
  - replace the alt="..." text with a real description of the photo
  - fill in show title, date, venue, credits, and notes in that pin-card block

Duplicate a whole <article class="pin-card">...</article> block per show.
